What we care about

Three rules we hold ourselves to in every app we ship.

Pay once

A fair one-time price instead of a monthly bill. You buy the app, and it stays yours.

Private by default

No accounts, no analytics SDKs, no ad networks. Your data never leaves your device.

Designed to disappear
